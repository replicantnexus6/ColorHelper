Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q. Can I use the app offline?

A. Yes. All features can be used offline. The app does not communicate with any external servers.

Q. Is my personal information saved?

A. No. Personal information is not saved either on the device or on any server. While app settings and similar preferences may be stored on your device, they do not contain any personally identifiable information.

Q. Are there any advertisements or tracking?

A. No. The app does not use any advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or crash report transmission tools.

Q. Do I need to grant permissions (Camera, Microphone, Location, etc.)?

A. No. This app does not request any of those permissions.

Q. Can I back up my data?

A. No personal information is stored within the app. If you need to back up settings or preferences, please use your device's system backup feature (iCloud or local backup).

Q. What should I do if a problem occurs?

A. Please try restarting your device or reinstalling the app. If the issue persists, contact us through the "Contact Us" section below with the details (steps to reproduce, device model, OS version, and app version).

User Guide (Quick Start)

  1. Launch the app.
  2. Use the color picker on the main screen to choose your desired color.
  3. You can search for colors defined by JIS standards from the color list.

Known Issues & Limitations

  • If you find any bugs or glitches, please let us know via the contact form/email.
  • The appearance of colors may vary depending on your device's display settings (such as True Tone or Night Shift).
